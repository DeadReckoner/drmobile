1. Introduction

Dead Reckoner LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") operates the Dead Reckoner mobile application ("App" or "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner. This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our App, as well as any related services, sales, marketing, or events. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Contact information (such as name, email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials (such as username and password; stored securely using hashing and encryption practices)
  • User profile information (such as profile picture, preferences)
  • Content you create and store within the App (e.g., goals, notes, uploads)

Usage and Technical Information

  • Device information (such as device type, operating system, IP address, mobile identifiers)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent, interaction events, crash reports)
  • Location information (if you grant permission, general location based on device settings)
  • Push notification tokens (if you opt in to receive notifications)
  • Log data (access times, hardware and software information)

Information from Third Parties

  • App Store or Google Play account information related to purchases/subscriptions
  • Information from social media accounts if you choose to connect them
  • Information from third-party services that integrate with our App
  • Information from business partners, such as payment processors

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Direct Interactions: Information you provide when you register, create a profile, submit forms, or communicate with us
  • Automated Technologies: Information collected automatically through app analytics tools, crash reporting, and device identifiers (instead of browser cookies)
  • Third Parties: Information we receive from app stores, connected services, or partners

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Improve, personalize, and expand the App
  • Understand and analyze how you use the App
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Communicate with you about updates, promotions, and support
  • Process your transactions and manage subscriptions
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies
  • Train and improve our AI-driven recommendations (using anonymized or aggregated data only)

Legal Bases for Processing (if applicable):

  • Consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate interests (balanced against your rights)
  • Legal obligations

5. Mobile Identifiers & Tracking Technologies

Instead of browser cookies, the App may use:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android)
  • In-app analytics tools to understand usage and performance
  • Push notification tokens to deliver messages to your device

You can manage tracking permissions and reset identifiers through your device settings.

6. Data Storage and Security

Data Retention: We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Generally, we retain your data for the duration of your active account and up to 12 months after account closure or deletion.

We may retain certain data for longer periods when required by law, including:

  • Financial and transaction records (up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Data necessary for ongoing legal proceedings or dispute resolution
  • Information required for regulatory compliance or law enforcement requests
  • Anonymized or aggregated data used for analytics and service improvement (indefinitely)

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting info@deadreckoner.io. Certain information may be retained where legally required.

7. Data Breach Response

We maintain security measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and privacy, we will:

  • Investigate and contain the incident promptly
  • Notify affected users via email or in-app notification within 72 hours of discovery, where feasible
  • Provide clear information about what data was involved, the likely consequences, and steps we are taking to address the breach
  • Report the incident to relevant authorities as required by applicable law
  • Take measures to prevent similar incidents in the future

We encourage you to monitor your account for any suspicious activity and report concerns to us immediately at info@deadreckoner.io.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you certain rights regarding your personal data. These rights may include:

14. Do Not Track & Preferences

Because mobile apps do not use browser cookies in the same way, “Do Not Track” signals are not applicable. You can manage permissions (location, notifications, tracking) directly in your device settings.

15. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including:

  • Requesting disclosure of data collected, sources, and purposes
  • Requesting deletion of personal information
  • Knowing with whom your data is shared
  • Exercising these rights without discrimination

Additionally, under California’s “Shine the Light” law (Civil Code § 1798.83), California residents may request information about the disclosure of their personal data to third parties for direct marketing purposes. Please note that we do not share personal information with third parties for their direct marketing without your consent. To exercise your rights, please contact us at info@deadreckoner.io.
